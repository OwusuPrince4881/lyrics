    About & sources

    Lyrics Sync uses these projects and services:

    • lrclib — direct lyric lookup
    • AcoustID and MusicBrainz — audio identification and recording metadata
    • Chromaprint — audio fingerprinting
    • Whisper / faster-whisper — local speech transcription
    • Demucs — local vocal separation

    Lyrics belong to their respective owners. This app does not host lyrics; it retrieves or transcribes them for display on your device.
